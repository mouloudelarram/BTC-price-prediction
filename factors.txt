### 1.2 Bond Markets & Interest Rates

**US Treasury Yields**
- **10-Year Treasury Yield**: Risk-free rate benchmark; inverse relationship with BTC during yield surges
  - *Impact*: Large, inverse correlation typically
  - *Timeframe*: Short to long-term
  - *Data Source*: US Treasury, Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)

- **2-Year Treasury Yield**: Short-term rate expectations
  - *Impact*: Large, reflects near-term Fed policy
  - *Timeframe*: Short-term
  - *Data Source*: FRED, Treasury Direct

- **30-Year Treasury Yield**: Long-term inflation expectations
  - *Impact*: Medium, reflects long-term economic outlook
  - *Timeframe*: Long-term
  - *Data Source*: FRED, Treasury Direct

- **2-Year/10-Year Yield Curve Spread**: Recession predictor; inverted curve signals risk-off
  - *Impact*: Large, recession indicator affects risk appetite
  - *Timeframe*: Medium to long-term
  - *Data Source*: FRED (calculated)

**Global Government Bonds**
- **German Bunds (10-Year)**: European risk-free rate

  - *Timeframe*: Short to medium-term

- **Japanese Government Bonds (JGB)**: Yield curve control policy impacts

  - *Timeframe*: Medium-term

- **UK Gilts**: British economic health and policy

  - *Timeframe*: Short-term

- **Investment-Grade Corporate Bond Spreads**: Overall credit market health

**Interest Rates**

  - *Impact*: Very large, fundamental driver

- **Federal Reserve Rate Expectations (Fed Futures)**: Market pricing of future rate changes

- **ECB Deposit Rate**: European monetary policy

## CONCLUSION

This comprehensive list covers **over 300+ distinct factors** that can influence Bitcoin prices across financial markets, macroeconomics, politics, crypto-specific metrics, sentiment, and indirect variables. Factors range from:

- **Very Large Impact**: Fed interest rates, major regulatory changes, halving events, institutional ETF flows, large exchange hacks
- **Large Impact**: Stock indices correlation, inflation data, stablecoin supply, exchange flows, derivatives markets
- **Medium Impact**: Regional economic data, altcoin performance, on-chain metrics, social sentiment
- **Small Impact**: Individual country elections (outside major economies), minor mining hardware releases, niche sentiment indicators

### Implementation Notes:
1. **Prioritize data collection** based on impact size and data availability
2. **Correlation does not imply causation** - many factors move together
3. **Time lags** exist between factor changes and BTC price impact
4. **Factor importance varies** by market regime (bull vs bear, high vs low volatility)
5. **Combine multiple factors** for robust analysis rather than relying on single indicators
6. **Continuously update** the factor list as markets evolve and new data becomes available

This list serves as a foundation for quantitative modeling, sentiment analysis, or fundamental research on Bitcoin price dynamics.
